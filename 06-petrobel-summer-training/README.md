# Summer Training – Belayim Petroleum Company (PETROBEL)

I successfully completed a Summer Training Program at Belayim Petroleum Company (PETROBEL).

The training provided practical exposure to the company's working environment and included activities across different departments as part of the summer training program for university and higher institute students.

- **Organization:** Belayim Petroleum Company (PETROBEL)
- **Training Type:** Summer Training

- **University:** Al-Azhar University
- **College:** Faculty of Science
- **Specialization:** Geology
- **Academic Level:** Second Year
- **Evaluation:** Excellent

## Training Program

The training program included exposure to different activities and departments within the company, including:

- Training and orientation
- Production activities
- Laboratory activities
- Training follow-up
- Completion of the training program

## Experience

The training provided practical exposure to the petroleum industry environment and helped connect academic studies with practical workplace experience.

It also provided an opportunity to observe different operational activities within a petroleum company and gain a better understanding of the professional working environment.
