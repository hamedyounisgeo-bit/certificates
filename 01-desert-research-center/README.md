# Summer Training – Desert Research Center

## Overview

I successfully completed a Summer Training Program at the Desert Research Center, under the Training Department.

The training was conducted within the Water Resources and Desert Lands Division and provided practical exposure to the field and working environment related to water resources and desert lands.

## Training Details

- **Organization:** Desert Research Center
- **Department:** Training Department
- **Training Division:** Water Resources and Desert Lands
- **Training Type:** Summer Training
- **Duration:** 21 July 2019 – 25 July 2019
- **University:** Al-Azhar University – Assiut
- **College:** Faculty of Science
- **Academic Level:** Second Year

## Training Experience

The training provided an opportunity to gain practical exposure to the activities and working environment of the Water Resources and Desert Lands Division.

It also helped bridge the gap between academic study and practical experience by providing exposure to the professional environment within a specialized research center.

## Key Areas

- Water Resources
- Desert Lands
- Practical Training
- Scientific Research Environment
- Field Exposure
