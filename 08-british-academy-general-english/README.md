# General English – British Academy for Training

## Overview

I successfully completed a General English training program provided by the British Academy for Training in collaboration with Al-Azhar University – Assiut Branch.

The program covered General English Levels 1, 2, and 3 and included 30 hours of training.

## Training Details

- **Training Provider:** British Academy for Training
- **In Collaboration With:** Al-Azhar University – Assiut Branch
- **Course:** General English
- **Levels:** 1, 2, and 3
- **Training Hours:** 30 Hours
- **Duration:** 26 February 2018 – 11 April 2018
- **Evaluation:** Very Good

## Learning Focus

The training focused on developing general English language skills through a structured multi-level training program.

## Experience

Completing the three-level General English program provided additional language training alongside my academic studies and helped develop my English communication skills.
