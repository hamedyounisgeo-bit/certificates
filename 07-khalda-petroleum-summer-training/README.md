# Summer Training – Khalda Petroleum Company

## Overview

I successfully completed a Summer Training Program at Khalda Petroleum Company as part of the company's summer training program for university and higher institute students.

The training provided practical exposure to the petroleum industry environment and helped connect academic study with practical application.

## Training Details

- **Organization:** Khalda Petroleum Company
- **Training Type:** Summer Training
- **Duration:** 6 July 2019 – 13 July 2019
- **Target Group:** University and Higher Institute Students
- **Evaluation:** Excellent

## Training Objectives

The summer training program was designed to:

- Enhance students' practical skills.
- Increase their knowledge through practical exposure.
- Connect academic study with practical application.
- Provide exposure to the professional work environment.

## Experience

This training provided practical exposure to the working environment of a petroleum company and helped strengthen the connection between academic knowledge and real-world professional practice.
